# Frame — charge-owner-scope (master row 31, change B of `specs/30-due-charges-owner-scope.md`)

OpenSpec change `openspec/changes/charge-owner-scope/`. Rules: WF-3, WF-9, ROLE-1..4, TEST-1..5.

## Summary

The member filter on donut and Sankey ignores who a charge belongs to. A charge gets an optional owner member or a « common » flag (default: follows its account), and the dashboard member scope gains per-account sub-chips (`?compte=`).

## HUMAN GATE (GIT-7)

The Prisma migration needs the human go-ahead before task 1. The Executor starts only after the PM logs it in `design.md`.
