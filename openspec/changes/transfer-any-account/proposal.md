# Proposal — transfer-any-account (row 22)

Why: a transfer is forced CHF -> EUR. Moving money between two accounts of the same currency (savings to current, any member's account to the joint account) needs a detour through an EUR account.

What changes for the user (any household member): a transfer can go between any two distinct open accounts. Same currency: no rate, no fee, credited at once (COMPLETED). Different currency (either direction): rate and fees as today (ESTIMATED, then credit).

Out of scope: Sankey/consolidated treatment of transfers (rows 23, 24), schema change (none), rate suggestions.
Unblocks: 23, 24.
